Questions

Frequently Asked

What's included in each set?
Every set includes 24 pieces of sushi, a curated selection of our signature sauces, wasabi, pickled ginger, premium soy sauce, chopsticks, and a collectible presentation insert. Omakase tier includes additional premium garnishes.
How far do you deliver?
We deliver across all of Batumi. During checkout you'll pin your exact location on the map. Delivery is included in the set price for addresses within the city limits. Maximum delivery time is 60 minutes.
Do you use real wasabi?
Our Signature and Omakase tiers include freshly prepared wasabi from imported roots — not the standard horseradish-based paste. Classic tier includes our premium house-blended wasabi.
Can I customize a set?
Omakase sets can be customized based on dietary preferences. For Classic and Signature tiers, we maintain the curated composition, but you can note allergies. Contact us via WhatsApp for special requests.
Why only 5 sets per day?
Quality over quantity. Each set is handcrafted to order using sashimi-grade ingredients. Limiting production ensures every piece meets our standards. Pre-order recommended.
